# Permissions

**Deprecated.** `Permission` and `PermissionGroup` are replaced by Scopes, Roles and RoleBindings (`overview.md`).

- These specifications don't define how Permissions behave, and no requirement in them depends on Permissions.
- Where a specification still mentions Permissions, the mention isn't a requirement, and isn't a reason to keep or change any other rule.
- Designs and reviews of these specifications ignore Permissions.
